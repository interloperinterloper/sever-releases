se/ver

Splits a song into vocals and music on your own GPU. Also finds its BPM and key.

DownloadLooking for the latest version...

About

Drop in a song and sever gives you two tracks: the vocals and the music. An open AI model does the work on your own graphics card, about 10 seconds for a normal song. Nothing is uploaded, so it works offline and unreleased tracks stay on your computer. Each track gets a waveform where you can mute it while the song plays, plus its own download button, and your past splits are kept in a history. It also reads the BPM and key, and shows both tempos (for example 86 / 172) when half-time is possible.

How it works

  1. Run the installer. It sets everything up and downloads the AI model on first start.
  2. Drop a song into the window.
  3. Listen, mute, download or open an old split from the history.

Needs

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card (RTX 50-series supported). Without one it still works, just slowly.
  • About 4 GB of disk space and internet for the first setup
