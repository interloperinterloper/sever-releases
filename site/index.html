Split any song.

Drop in a song and get the vocals and the music as separate tracks, plus BPM and key. Runs on your own GPU, free and offline.

How it works

  1. Run the installer. It sets everything up and downloads the AI model on first start.
  2. Drop a song into the window.
  3. Mute either track while it plays, download vocals or music, or open an old split from your history.

Needs

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card (RTX 50-series supported). Without one it still works, just slowly.
  • About 4 GB of disk space and an internet connection for the first setup
